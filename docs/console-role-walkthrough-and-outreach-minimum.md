## Cross-role finish checks

1. Owner changes scope or visibility. The previous session must fail; a fresh session must observe the reduced permissions. Historical record assignment must not restore revoked scope.
2. Change role: prior sessions stop, previous actor-role snapshots remain unchanged, and recertification is required.
3. Suspend/deactivate: Console access stops and identity/history remain. Record pending edge revocation honestly; separately verify old and fresh edge-session denial through the approved execution path.
4. Finalize report v1, change permitted current facts, then append linked v2 with a reason. Verify both snapshots and original attribution remain; unauthorized alteration/deletion must fail. Re-read history under a restricted current role.
5. For Step 20, use only an explicitly approved isolated TRAINING source target and transport. The present TRAINING route denies production synchronization. Do not remove that fence or point the test at the operational workbook.
6. Under that authorized source setup, retain import → permitted change → export → source acknowledgment → reimport evidence. A pending export must survive a conflicting import; replay must not duplicate the audit event. Preserve Product Focus / Product Scope and protected formulas/columns.
7. For Step 21, retain each finalized report version and evidence artifact in the Owner-selected protected destination. Compare an independently restored copy, source/configuration and hashes. Local manifests do not prove live storage retention or remote recovery.
